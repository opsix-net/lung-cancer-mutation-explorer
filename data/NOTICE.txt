This snapshot selects BRAF, EGFR, KEAP1, KRAS, STK11, and TP53 from the mutation
profile. It retains all returned records for those genes in the sequenced sample
list, selects nine fields, sorts the rows, and uses Not_reported for a missing
protein-change annotation. Coordinates are on GRCh37. It contains 803 mutation
records and 566 study sample IDs. Retrieval date: 2026-11-09 UTC.

Counts describe recorded mutations and distinct sample IDs, not population
prevalence or personal risk. An absent record does not prove biological absence.
This six-gene subset does not represent all genomic alterations in the study.
